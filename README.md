# Superstore Data Analysis 📊

## Overview

This project analyzes the **Superstore dataset** to answer three business questions:

* Find the most profitable products/categories
* Identify the best-performing region
* Find products with high sales but low profit

The analysis was performed using **Python and Pandas**, with **Matplotlib** used for visualization.

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project is the **Superstore Dataset Final**, available on Kaggle:

**[Superstore Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)**

The dataset contains **9,994 retail order records** with information about sales, products, categories, regions, discounts, and profits.

## Analysis & Findings

### 1. Most Profitable Products and Categories

Profit was aggregated by **Category** and **Product Name**.

* **Technology** was the most profitable category.
* **Canon image CLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the most profitable product.

### 2. Best-Performing Region

Sales, Quantity, and Profit were aggregated by region. The **Coefficient of Variation (CV)** was calculated to compare the relative variation of these measures.

* **West** was the best-performing region based on total profit.
* **Profit** had the highest CV, indicating greater relative variation across regions than Sales or Quantity.

### 3. High Sales but Low Profit

Products were analyzed using:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Average Discount
* Profit Margin

Profit Margin was calculated as:

`Profit Margin = Profit / Sales × 100`

Several products had negative profit margins. The **top 10 products with the most negative profit margins** were identified for further review.

Among the high-sales products, the **Cisco TelePresence System EX90 Videoconferencing Unit** stood out because it generated substantial sales while still having a negative profit margin.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Focus on highly profitable products and categories**, especially Technology and products such as the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier, by maintaining inventory and using targeted promotions.

2. **Strengthen the West region strategy** because it generates the highest total profit, while investigating lower-performing regions to identify opportunities for improvement.

3. **Review pricing and discount strategies** for high-sales, low-profit products such as the Cisco TelePresence System EX90 to reduce losses and improve profit margins.

## Mystery Question

**What looked successful but wasn't?**

The **Cisco TelePresence System EX90 Videoconferencing Unit** looked successful because of its high sales, but it wasn't profitable because it had a negative profit margin.
